How to get honest feedback on your prompts from your own AI

Quick answer: open the chat, click Export, and under Improve your prompts click Copy review request. Paste it into a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and send it. Your AI tells you where the chat went off track and rewrites your first message so it gets things right the first time.

Why review a chat?

Most long AI chats go wrong in the first message: a missing goal, audience or output format, so the next ten messages are corrections. Seeing that on one of your own chats teaches more than a list of generic prompt tips.

What you get back

  1. What you were trying to achieve
  2. What went well
  3. Where it went off track — with short quotes from the chat
  4. What your first message was missing (goal, context, audience, constraints, examples, format)
  5. Up to 5 questions to answer before starting
  6. A better first message, with [placeholders] where only you know the details
  7. Three tips for next time

The AI replies in the language you wrote in.

The one-click way

  1. Install AI Chat Exporter: ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini (free).
  2. Open the chat on chatgpt.com, claude.ai or gemini.google.com and click Export.
  3. Under Improve your prompts, click Copy review request.

Then paste into a new chat and send it. You can review the whole chat or only some messages: pick them first under Messages. Very long chats may be too long for some AIs — pick fewer messages if yours says so.

Without the extension

Copy the conversation yourself and paste it under this request in a new chat:

Please review how I used AI in the conversation below. Help me get better results next time. Be specific, kind and practical, and quote short parts of the conversation as evidence. Reply in the language I wrote in, with these sections: 1. What I was trying to achieve 2. What went well 3. Where it went off track 4. What my first message was missing 5. Questions to answer before starting 6. A better first message (Goal, Context, Constraints, Output format; leave [placeholders] instead of inventing facts) 7. Three tips for next time. Only review the conversation — don't continue it.

Is it private?

The extension only copies text to your clipboard; you decide where to paste it. Nothing is sent to us — there is no CarryAll server, no account and no analytics. The review then happens in your own AI account, under that service's privacy terms. Details in the privacy policy.

Questions

Does the extension send my chat anywhere?

No. It copies the review request to your clipboard. You paste and send it yourself, to the AI of your choice.

Which AI should do the review?

Any of them: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. A new chat works best, so the review is not mixed with the old conversation.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no limits, like single-chat export.

Can I review only part of a chat?

Yes. Choose "Pick messages" in the Export window first, then click Copy review request.
