Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: every first purchase comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee, and you can cancel the monthly plan at any time.

14-day money-back guarantee

If Pro isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of your first purchase of either plan (Pro Monthly or Pro Lifetime) and we'll refund it in full. No questions asked.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email siddheshthapa02@gmail.com from the address you used to buy, or include your order ID from the receipt.
  2. We'll confirm within 2 business days.
  3. The refund is issued by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record, to your original payment method. How long it takes to appear depends on your bank or card provider.

Once a purchase is refunded, its licence key stops working.

Cancelling Pro Monthly

  • Cancel anytime, using the link in your Dodo Payments receipt email or by emailing us.
  • Cancelling stops all future charges. You keep Pro until the end of the month you've already paid for.
  • After the first 14 days, monthly payments already made are not refunded, unless the law where you live requires it.

Pro Lifetime

Refundable in full within 14 days of purchase. After that, lifetime purchases are not refundable, unless the law where you live requires it.

If something isn't working

Please email us before opening a dispute with your bank — most problems can be fixed quickly, and if we can't fix a problem with a paid feature, we'll refund you.
