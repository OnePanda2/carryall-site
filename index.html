Questions

Is this an official OpenAI, Anthropic or Google product?

No. It's an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google.

Where do my chats go?

Nowhere. The extension reads the chat you're looking at, builds the file on your computer, and saves it to your Downloads folder. We never receive your chats.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and click Export. Pro is unlocked with a licence key that's emailed to you after purchase.

Chrome says “Proceed with caution” when I install it. Is it safe?

Chrome shows that message only to people who turned on Enhanced protection, for extensions from publishers who are new to the Chrome Web Store. It isn’t a malware warning: Google says that for new developers “it generally takes a few months to become trusted” (Google’s explanation). We published our first version in October 2026. The extension runs only on chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com, and your chats never reach us — see the Privacy section of the store page and our privacy policy. If you’re happy with that, click Continue to install.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. Firefox is planned.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes. Cancel the monthly plan anytime, and every first purchase has a 14-day money-back guarantee. Details in the refund and cancellation policy.
